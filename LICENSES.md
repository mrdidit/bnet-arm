# Licenses

BNet distributes components under their existing upstream licenses. It does not replace them with a single project-wide license.

- Wine retains its LGPL-2.1-or-later license and copyright notices.
- FEX and its dependencies retain the licenses included with their source trees.
- GE-Proton and the remaining runtime components retain their upstream notices.
- Original patch authorship is preserved in the patch files and source records.

The runtime archive contains the base tool's licenses plus collected notices under `bnet/licenses/`. The release's three source archives contain the corresponding prepared component sources, unchanged GE sources, build recipes and license files. [Source information](SOURCES.md).

The repository's patch copies retain the licensing and attribution of their source projects. Redistribution must preserve the applicable notices and source obligations.
